In His Grip Ministries — 2026 Vision & Ministry Highlights

Founded by Jeff and Connie Johnson, In His Grip Ministries is dedicated to crisis intervention, altar ministry, addiction recovery outreach, and equipping men and women for enduring restoration.

🔥

From Bondage to Freedom

After 28 years bound by addiction and prison sentences, Jeff encountered the genuine transforming touch of God in 2013—breaking chains for good.

🤝

Walking Alongside Others

Serving across Texas, Mississippi, Alabama, and Florida, bringing personal mentorship, love, and hope to individuals and families at their lowest point.

📖

Power of the Gospel

Guiding, loving, caring, and pointing every weary soul toward Jesus Christ through biblical truth and compassionate ministry.
